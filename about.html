Meet the founder

Mr. Syed Naseer Ahmad Andrabi

Founder & Proprietor

Mr. Andrabi began this journey in 2000 with a commitment to bring the best medical equipment and service to the healthcare sector of Jammu & Kashmir.

Under his leadership, Medico has grown from repairing electronic medical devices into an authorised partner for Philips, Schiller and Shalya, and a name hospitals across the region associate with quality, reliability and care.
