Our belief

Only the best solutions are good enough for saving lives. That belief has guided every product we supply, every installation we finish and every call we answer since 2000.

Our founder

Mr. Syed Naseer Ahmad Andrabi

Founder & Proprietor

Mr. Andrabi began this journey in 2000 with a commitment to bring the best medical equipment and service to the healthcare sector of Jammu & Kashmir. Under his leadership, Medico has become a name that stands for quality, reliability and customer satisfaction.

Our journey

From a repair bench to Kashmir’s trusted partner.

  1. 2000

    Foundation

    Started as a first-of-its-kind venture in Kashmir, repairing and selling electronic medical devices.

  2. Growth

    Specialising in life support

    Evolved into a trusted distributor of respiratory and life-support devices, and an authorised partner for Philips, Schiller and Shalya.

  3. Today

    Equipment and service across J&K

    Supplying, installing and servicing equipment for 100+ hospitals, clinics and families, with support seven days a week.
