Questions we hear often

Not covered here? Ask us directly. A specialist answers, not a call centre.

Ask a question
What medical equipment do you supply?

Oxygen concentrators, CPAP and BiPAP machines, patient monitors, anaesthesia workstations, defibrillators, diagnostic systems such as ECG and pulmonary function testing, and surgical instruments. We supply hospitals, nursing homes, clinics and patients at home across Jammu & Kashmir.

Which brands are you authorised for?

We are an authorised partner for Philips (respiratory and sleep therapy), Schiller (cardiology and diagnostics) and Shalya (surgical and OT systems). Authorised means genuine stock, manufacturer warranty and certified installation.

Do you install and service equipment outside Srinagar?

Yes. Our team delivers, installs and services equipment across Jammu & Kashmir. Tell us the district and the equipment, and we will confirm the visit schedule.

Do you offer annual maintenance contracts (AMC)?

Yes. We offer preventive maintenance, calibration and AMCs for the equipment we supply. Hospitals use them to keep devices compliant and avoid unplanned downtime.

Can you help set up oxygen or CPAP therapy at home?

Yes. We help families choose the right device, set it up at home and show everyone how to use and clean it. Your doctor decides the therapy and settings; we make sure the equipment delivers them.

Do you stock spare parts and consumables?

We supply genuine spare parts and common consumables for the brands we represent, such as masks, filters and tubing. Ask us for a specific part and we will check availability.

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form on this site, message us on WhatsApp or call +91 94190 08453. Share what you need and where it will be used, and a specialist will come back with options and pricing.

Where are you located, and when are you open?

Medico is on the 2nd Floor, Iqbal Complex, Opposite Iqbal Park, Shergarhi, Srinagar 190008. We are open seven days a week, 9 AM to 8 PM. Email: medicosgr@gmail.com.
