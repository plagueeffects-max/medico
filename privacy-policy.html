Medico Scientific Service Centre ("Medico", "we", "us", or "our"), located at 2nd Floor, Iqbal Complex, Opposite Iqbal Park, Central Market, Batamaloo, Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir 190009, operates the website https://medico-inky.vercel.app/. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023.

1. Data We Collect

We may collect the following personal data when you interact with our website:

  • Contact information: Name, email address, phone number — collected when you submit an enquiry form or contact us via email or WhatsApp.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent, browser type — collected automatically via standard web server logs.
  • Communications: Content of messages you send to us.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide customer support
  • To process orders and arrange product or service delivery
  • To send transactional communications related to your enquiry
  • To improve our website and services

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Your personal data is stored securely and retained only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Enquiry data is retained for a maximum of 3 years.

4. Your Rights Under DPDPA 2023

As a data principal under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data
  • Withdraw consent for processing at any time
  • Request erasure of your personal data
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at medicosgr@gmail.com.

5. Third-Party Services

Fonts and scripts for this website are served from our own domain. The article and product pages load a sanitising library from the jsDelivr CDN, and the About page embeds a Google Map, which Google may use to collect limited usage data under its own privacy policy. Enquiries you send through the website open in WhatsApp, which processes them under its own terms. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

6. Security

Our website is served over HTTPS. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss.
