Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Who we are What we collect How we use it Where it is stored and for how long Services we rely on Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023 Security Changes to this policy Contact

This policy explains what personal data this website collects, why, and the choices you have. It is written to follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Who we are

Medico Scientific Service Centre (“Medico”, “we”, “us”) supplies, installs and services medical equipment from 2nd Floor, Iqbal Complex, Opposite Iqbal Park, Shergarhi, Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir 190008. We are responsible for the personal data described here.

2. What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us. When you use the enquiry form we receive:

  • Your name and phone or WhatsApp number, so we can call you back.
  • Your email address, if you add it, for quotations and brochures.
  • Who you are enquiring for (hospital, nursing home, clinic, home care or other), the equipment topic and any details you type.
  • The page you sent it from, so we know which product you were looking at.

To stop spam and abuse we also store a one-way scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address and your browser type with each enquiry. The hash cannot be turned back into your IP address.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. If you save products on the catalogue page, that list stays in your own browser and is never sent to us.

3. How we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and send quotations, specifications and delivery timelines.
  • To arrange demonstrations, installation, training and engineer visits across Jammu & Kashmir.
  • To keep warranty and maintenance (AMC) records if you buy from us.

We never sell, rent or share your details with advertisers, and we do not send marketing messages you did not ask for.

4. Where it is stored and for how long

Enquiries travel over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. They are stored in a secure cloud database that only authorised Medico staff can open, through a password-protected admin area, and a copy is emailed to our team so we can respond quickly. We keep an enquiry for as long as we need it to serve you, including any warranty or maintenance that follows, and delete it sooner if you ask us to.

5. Services we rely on

  • Vercel hosts this website.
  • Neon provides the database where enquiries are stored.
  • Web3Forms delivers each enquiry to our team’s email inbox. It receives the details you entered in the form and passes them only to us.
  • Google Maps shows our location on the About page. Google may set its own cookies when the map loads, under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Google reviews shown on the site are fetched by our server; no visitor data is sent to Google for this.
  • WhatsApp: if you tap a WhatsApp button, the conversation happens in WhatsApp under Meta’s privacy policy.

6. Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, or withdraw the consent you gave when you sent the enquiry;
  • name someone to exercise these rights on your behalf.

Write to medicosgr@gmail.com or call +91 94190 08453. We aim to reply within 7 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Security

The whole site runs over HTTPS. The admin area is protected by a password and signed sessions, repeated failed sign-ins are blocked, and only authorised Medico staff can see enquiries.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data we will update this page and the date at the top. Please also read our Terms of Use.
