Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 May 2026

Data We Collect How We Use Your Data Data Storage and Retention Your Rights Under DPDPA 2023 Third-Party Services Security Contact Changes to This Policy

Medico Scientific Service Centre ("Medico", "we", "us", or "our"), located at 2nd Floor, Iqbal Complex, Opposite Iqbal Park, Shergarhi, Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir 190008, operates the website https://medico-inky.vercel.app/. This Privacy Policy explains strictly what information we collect when you interact with our website, why we collect it, and how it is protected under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023.

1. Data We Collect (Only What You Provide)

We do not collect unnecessary background data or track your browsing activity across other websites. When you submit a quote request or contact us for medical equipment support, we ask only for the essential details required to assist you:

  • Full Name: To address you and register your inquiry.
  • Phone Number: To call you back or WhatsApp you with quotes, specifications, and delivery timelines.
  • Email Address (Optional): To send formal quotations, manufacturer brochures, and warranty cards.
  • Organisation / Clinic Name (Optional): To understand your setup (hospital ICU, clinic, or home patient care).
  • Equipment Interest & Message: The specific medical device or service you need assistance with (such as oxygen concentrators, CPAP/BiPAP, ECG, or OT setup).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your details are used strictly for clinical equipment communication:

  • To respond directly to your quote request or technical inquiry.
  • To coordinate equipment demonstrations, delivery, and biomedical engineer visits across Jammu & Kashmir.
  • To maintain your warranty and Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) service records upon purchase.

Zero Spam & Zero Selling: We never sell, rent, or trade your contact information with advertisers or third-party marketing agencies. We do not send unsolicited promotional marketing messages.

3. Data Storage & Processing

Inquiries submitted via our web forms are securely transmitted via encrypted HTTPS and processed through our verified form gateway (Web3Forms) to reach our authorized technical support desk. Records are maintained in our secure, encrypted database solely for customer service history and manufacturer warranty fulfillment.

4. Your Rights Under DPDPA 2023

You have full ownership of your personal data. You may at any time:

  • Request a copy of the inquiry information we have on file for you.
  • Request immediate correction or updating of your contact details.
  • Request complete deletion of your past inquiry records from our system.

To exercise your rights, email us directly at medicosgr@gmail.com or phone +91 94190 08453.

5. Third-Party Services

We believe in minimal third-party footprint. Our website uses:

  • Web3Forms: Secure dispatch of inquiry forms without storing marketing trackers.
  • Google Maps Embed: To help visitors locate our service centre at Iqbal Complex, Srinagar.
  • WhatsApp Direct: If you choose to click our WhatsApp button, your conversation occurs directly within WhatsApp under Meta's privacy policy.

6. Security

All communication across this website is strictly encrypted using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS encryption. Access to customer inquiry records is restricted to verified Medico engineering personnel.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact:
