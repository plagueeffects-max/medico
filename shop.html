        Can I request a quotation for equipment?

        Yes. Request a quote here on the website, on WhatsApp or by phone. Our specialists recommend the right product and send a detailed quote based on your requirements.

        Do you provide installation and training?

        Yes. Equipment we supply includes on-site delivery, professional installation, testing and clinical training for nursing staff and biomedical engineers.

        Which areas do you serve?

        We serve districts across Jammu & Kashmir from our centre in Srinagar, including Baramulla, Anantnag, Pulwama, Kupwara and Jammu, with on-ground technical support.

        Can you help identify the right model for my facility?

        Yes. Our consultants look at your caseload, bed capacity, power infrastructure and budget, then recommend the right configuration.
