# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Public site: procurement staff, doctors and biomedical engineers at hospitals and clinics across Jammu & Kashmir, plus families buying home-care equipment (oxygen, sleep/NIV). They arrive from Google or WhatsApp, mostly on phones, and want to know: does Medico carry the brand/device, can they service it, and how do I reach them now.

Admin panel (product register): the business owner and staff, phone-first, non-technical. Jobs: edit site text, add products and blog posts, read enquiries.

## Product Purpose
Medico Scientific Service Centre is an established medical equipment supplier and service centre in J&K (authorised partner for brands such as Philips, Schiller, Shalya). The site turns searches into enquiries (call, WhatsApp, contact form) and proves credibility: years in business, brands, service capability, real installs. Success = more qualified enquiries, and an owner who can keep the site current without a developer.

## Brand Personality
Premium, clinical, modern. Calm confidence of a serious equipment partner; precise, unhurried, factual. No hype, no exclamation marks. Photography of real equipment and the real store carries warmth.

## Anti-references
- Generic medical templates: stock smiling-doctor heroes, teal/blue gradients, heart-pulse icons.
- SaaS landing grammar: eyebrow kicker over every section, hero-metric stat strips, identical icon-card grids.
- Glassmorphism, gradient text, decorative blobs.
- Repeating the same fact (year, brands, CTA) several times inside one viewport.

## Design Principles
1. Restraint signals quality: one accent, generous space, few weights.
2. Proof over claims: real brands, installs, photos and numbers instead of adjectives.
3. Every screen ends in a clear next step (call, WhatsApp, enquire), stated once.
4. Phone first: the J&K buyer is on a mid-range Android or iPhone, often on slow data.
5. The owner must be able to run it: admin is plain-language and forgiving.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Best effort, with sensible defaults: readable contrast on body text, 44px touch targets, keyboard-usable forms and dialogs, prefers-reduced-motion respected. Must work on iPhone Safari, Android Chrome and Windows Chrome.
